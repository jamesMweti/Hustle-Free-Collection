OUR STORY

Shopping 2.0 .

Shopping has become a more tiresome activity in the recent time especially to students. Hustle Free Collection gives you an opportunity to purchase your favourite Campus essentials without any hustle, so you get more time to explore and enjoy your stay in the Campus.

Our Goal

Reduce the hustle that has been there during shopping by bringing services to your doorstep.

Our value

Enable each and every of our customer have a reach to quality essentials for their daily endeavours.

Business model

This website generates revenue through sales commission, money received for delivery of the Orders.

Project scope

Allows a customer to be able to see the products offered, add them to cart and place an order. They can also remove the items from the cart. It does not however integrate any payment method.
